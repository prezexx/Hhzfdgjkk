Source: https://github.com/NinjaGuyDev/autoblow-panel  
License: MIT. Motion preset definitions are converted to the local pattern format. The upstream license is stored under `patterns/autoblow/`.

## Handy AI Motion
Source: https://github.com/Fran31416/handy-ai-motion

Source: https://github.com/hapticforge/wavedriver

Source: https://github.com/testerone321/The-Edgy  
License notices: MIT and ISC are both present upstream. Stateful movement concepts are adapted into deterministic HSP-compatible loops. Both notices are stored under `patterns/the-edgy/`.

Proprietary or unlicensed repositories discovered during research are not copied into this project.
